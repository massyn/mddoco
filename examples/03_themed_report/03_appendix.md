# Appendix

## Method

Latency figures are p50 values sampled at one-minute resolution and aggregated
per hour. Availability is measured from the external synthetic monitor.

## Glossary

| Term          | Meaning                                             |
|---------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Error budget  | Allowed unavailability within the target SLA        |
| p50 / p95     | 50th / 95th percentile of the measured distribution |
